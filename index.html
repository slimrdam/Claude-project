Sources and limits

  • SBET daily bars from the Yahoo Finance chart API, split-adjusted, with Stooq as fallback. ETH and BTC daily closes from CoinGecko, with CoinMarketCap's data API as fallback. Refreshed by a scheduled job; history accumulates in data.json so the archive outlives any single API's lookback window.
  • The green/red signal is SBET's daily return minus ether's, close to close, with weekend crypto moves folded into the following session.
  • The ratio index needs no assumptions about share count or holdings, so it is the reliable long series. The mNAV level does need them and is drawn for the last 90 days only.
  • ETH holdings are disclosure dates, not a daily series; the line between marks is drawn for readability, not measured.
  • Adoption figures and the ETH/BTC levels are hand-maintained in notes.json and carry their own review date above. They do not refresh with the price data.
